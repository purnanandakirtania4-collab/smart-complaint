1. Activation

Worker Pro becomes active only after successful Razorpay authorisation/payment and server-side verification. An incomplete or failed payment does not activate paid access.

2. AutoPay

Every Worker Pro plan is recurring. Monthly renews at ₹149 after its ₹49 introductory first month. The 4-month plan renews at ₹589 every 4 months. The yearly plan renews at ₹1769 every 12 months.

3. AI credits

AI credits are controlled in rolling 30-day cycles: 150 for Monthly, 155 for 4 Months and 160 for Yearly. Unused credits do not accumulate into a larger future allowance.

4. Current features

Current Worker Pro AI tools include Complaint Summary, Work Checklist and Reply Draft. AI output is optional and never automatically changes complaint status, verifies work, or sends a reply on the worker's behalf.

5. Future features

Any feature described as planned, future or early access is not guaranteed to be available today. Smart Complaint may add, change or discontinue future benefits while keeping current paid terms clear.

6. Failed recurring payment

If a renewal fails because of insufficient balance, bank or UPI restrictions, expired authorisation or another payment issue, paid access may stop according to the verified subscription state.

7. Renewal management

The worker may turn off future renewal using Smart Complaint where available or through the applicable payment provider. When cycle-end cancellation is successfully scheduled, already-paid access continues until the current paid period ends.

8. Refunds and provider rules

Processed payments may be non-refundable except where Smart Complaint approves a refund or applicable law requires one. Razorpay, the worker's bank and UPI/card provider may apply their own payment and mandate rules.

Important: By continuing, you are authorising a recurring plan. Check the plan price and renewal frequency on the Razorpay screen before approving the mandate.
