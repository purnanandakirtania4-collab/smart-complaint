How AI credits work

Monthly: 150 credits per 30-day AI cycle.
4 Months: 155 credits per 30-day AI cycle.
Yearly: 160 credits per 30-day AI cycle.

Credits reset by 30-day cycle instead of being issued all at once. This keeps AI usage controlled across longer subscriptions. Cached duplicate results can still be reused without a new credit charge when eligible.
Current and planned features: Summary, Checklist and Reply Draft are current features. Any item marked planned is only a future direction and is not part of the guaranteed service today.
