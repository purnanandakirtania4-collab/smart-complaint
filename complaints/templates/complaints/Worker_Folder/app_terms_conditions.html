Worker App Terms & Conditions

These rules explain the responsibilities of workers while using the Smart Complaint platform and while handling customer complaints.

Worker: {{ worker.name }}
Worker ID: {{ worker.worker_id }}
This is very important notice for you.

1. Use the App Responsibly

Use the Smart Complaint worker account only for genuine service work. Do not misuse the app, create fake activity, or use another person's worker account.

2. Provide Correct Profile Information

Keep your name, phone number, email, experience, profile photo, and other worker information accurate and up to date.

3. Handle Complaints Honestly

Accept and work on complaints honestly. Do not mark a complaint as completed unless the required work has actually been completed.

4. Respect Customers

Communicate politely and professionally. Abusive language, threats, harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behaviour toward a customer is not allowed.

5. Protect Customer Privacy

Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, complaint details, photos, location information, chat messages, and other personal information must be used only for completing the assigned work. Do not share or misuse this information.

6. Use Chat Only for Complaint Work

The complaint chat should be used only for communication related to the assigned complaint. Spam, advertising, harassment, or unrelated messages are not allowed.

7. Before and After Photos Must Be Genuine

Upload only genuine work photos related to the complaint. Do not upload copied, misleading, unrelated, or manipulated photos as proof of work.

8. Completion OTP Must Not Be Misused

The completion OTP is used to confirm that the customer agrees the complaint can be completed. Do not pressure the customer, guess the OTP, obtain it dishonestly, or misuse it.

9. Location Information Must Be Used Carefully

Complaint location information is provided only to help complete the assigned service. Do not store, share, track, or use a customer's location for unrelated purposes.

10. Do Not Manipulate Ratings

Do not pressure customers to give a particular rating, create fake ratings, or attempt to manipulate the rating system.

11. Follow Complaint Priority Properly

High Priority and Emergency complaints should be treated with appropriate urgency. Do not falsely change, ignore, or misuse priority information.

12. Do Not Share Account Access

Keep your login credentials private. You are responsible for activity performed through your worker account.

13. Follow Applicable Safety Rules

Perform service work safely and only within your skills and ability. Do not perform work that you are not qualified or equipped to complete safely.

14. Fraud and False Information Are Not Allowed

Fake complaints, false completion claims, fake work evidence, impersonation, payment fraud, or any other fraudulent activity may result in action against the worker account.

15. Account Action for Serious Violations

Smart Complaint may restrict, suspend, or review a worker account when there is evidence of serious misuse, fraud, harassment, repeated rule violations, or activity that creates risk for users or the platform.

16. Rules May Be Updated

These app rules may be updated when the platform changes or when additional safety, privacy, or service requirements are needed. Workers should review the latest version available in Settings.

Important: These App Terms & Conditions are separate from the Worker Subscription Terms & Conditions. Subscription pricing, AutoPay, payment, cancellation, and billing rules are available only from the Subscription section.
